Are Custom Foot Orthotics Worth It? And How Long Do They Last?

September 10, 20265 min readFoot Care

Foot pain can wear you down. It affects how far you walk, how long you can stand and even your mood. So it’s no surprise that many people ask us: “Are custom foot orthotics worth the money?”

The honest answer is: it depends on what’s causing your pain. Here’s how to work out if they’re right for you.

What are custom foot orthotics?

Custom orthotics are insoles made from the shape of your own feet. An orthotist checks your feet, watches how you walk and takes a cast or impression. The insole is then made to support your foot in a better position.

They’re not the same as gel insoles or arch supports from a shop. Those are made for an “average” foot.

Who they can help

Custom foot orthotics often help people with:

  • Heel pain and plantar fasciitis. This is pain under the heel, often worst with your first steps in the morning.
  • Flat feet that ache, tire easily or roll inwards a lot.
  • High arches that put extra pressure on the heel and ball of the foot.
  • Diabetic or sensitive feet, where spreading pressure can help protect the skin.
  • A difference in leg length, which can affect your hips and back.
  • Knee, hip or back pain that seems linked to the way your feet move.

When a shop insole might be enough

Custom isn’t always needed. A good ready-made insole may be fine if:

  • Your pain is mild and only comes on after long days
  • Your feet are fairly “average” in shape
  • You simply want a bit more cushioning

If you’ve tried shop insoles and they haven’t helped, or your problem is more complex, custom orthotics are worth considering. A good orthotist will tell you honestly which they think you need.

Custom vs store-bought: a quick comparison

Store-bought insoles Custom orthotics
Made from Set sizes and shapes A cast or scan of your feet
Fit General Matches your arches and heels
Problem solving Basic support or cushioning Made for your specific problem
Lifespan Often months Often a year or more

How long do custom orthotics last?

It depends on the material, your weight and how active you are. Many custom orthotics last a year or more. Some firmer types last longer.

Signs it’s time for a check or a new pair:

  • The top cover is worn through
  • The shell is cracked or has gone flat
  • Your old pain is coming back
  • Your feet, weight or activity have changed a lot

Getting used to them

Most people need one to two weeks to get used to new orthotics. Here’s what helps:

  1. Wear them for an hour or two on the first day.
  2. Add a little more time each day.
  3. Expect some new feelings in your feet or legs at first.
  4. Stop and call us if you get blisters or sharp pain.

So, are they worth it?

For the right person, yes. If your foot pain is affecting your daily life and it’s linked to your foot shape or the way you walk, custom orthotics can make standing and walking much more comfortable.

The key is a proper assessment first. Find out more on our custom foot orthotics page, or book a foot check.

This article is general information, not medical advice. Please see your doctor about foot pain that’s severe, sudden, or comes with swelling, numbness or skin changes.
