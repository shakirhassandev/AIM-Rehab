Comprehensive Prosthetic, Orthotic & Rehabilitation Solutions

A complete range of mobility and support solutions for adults and children, all made to your measurements and fitted by certified prosthetists and orthotists in Rawalpindi.

Featured Service

Lower Limb Prosthetics

Customized artificial limbs for people with lower limb loss, designed for comfort, function and an active lifestyle. Below-knee and above-knee legs are built around a socket that fits you well.

  • Individualized design and fit
  • Functional for daily activities
  • Support for mobility and independence

AFO, KAFO & HKAFO Braces

Custom leg braces that support weak muscles, control the ankle or knee and help with problems like foot drop.

Spinal & Fracture Bracing

Braces for scoliosis, back injuries and healing fractures, fitted to hold you in the right position without rubbing.
