Good To Know

Before your visit

  • Bring any doctor's letters, scan reports and a list of your medicines.
  • Bring the shoes you wear most and any device you use now.
  • Wear loose clothes so we can easily see the area we need to check.
  • For children, please bring a parent or carer and a favorite toy or snack.
