A Trusted Prosthetic & Orthotic Clinic in Rawalpindi

Restoring mobility, building independence and improving quality of life through personalized, specialist-led care for adults and children.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, personalized prosthetic, orthotic and rehabilitation care that helps people move better, live independently and take part fully in daily life.

Who We Are

AIM Rehab is a prosthetic and orthotic clinic in Rawalpindi. We make and fit artificial limbs, leg braces, spinal braces and foot orthotics for adults and children.

Our clinic is led by Ali Hassan and Khurram Niazi, ISPO certified prosthetists and orthotists. Every device starts with a proper check of your body and your goals, then we build it to your measurements.

Why Fit Matters

Many people end up with a device that doesn't quite fit. It rubs, feels heavy or nobody showed them how to use it, so it ends up in a cupboard. A well-fitted device improves comfort, function and long-term health.

Our Care Philosophy

We believe the fitting is only half the job. The other half is listening, teaching and coming back to check. Our care is person-centred and focused on practical outcomes: better mobility, greater independence and confidence in everyday life.

We explain your options in everyday words. You'll always know what we suggest and why, before anything is made.

Our Promises

  • Quality & SafetyClinically appropriate and reliable solutions
  • Personalized CareSolutions tailored to your unique needs
  • Ongoing SupportFollow-up, adjustments and long-term care
  • Respect & DignityA caring, inclusive and supportive experience

Our Care Process

  1. 01

    Assessment

    We listen to your goals and check your limb, joints and the way you move.

  2. 02

    Custom Plan

    We explain your options in plain words and design a device for you.

  3. 03

    Fitting & Training

    You try it before it is finished, then learn to use it with confidence.

  4. 04

    Follow-Up

    We check comfort and fit as your body changes, for as long as you need.

Made-to-Measure Care

Your body is not a standard size, so your device should not be either. Every device is made to your shape, so it fits the way you live.

Follow-Up Support

Our care doesn't end at fitting. Bodies change and children grow, so we keep checking comfort and fit long after the first day.
