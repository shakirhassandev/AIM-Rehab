Why It Matters

Why fit matters so much to us

Many people who need an artificial limb or a brace end up with a device that doesn't quite fit. It rubs, it feels heavy or nobody showed them how to use it properly. So it ends up in a cupboard.

At AIM Rehab, we believe the fitting is only half the job. The other half is listening, teaching and coming back to check. Our certified prosthetists and orthotists work this way with every patient.

We make and fit prosthetic legs and arms, AFO, KAFO and HKAFO leg braces, spinal and fracture braces, children's orthotics and custom foot orthotics. Every device is made for one person, and we stay in touch as that person's needs change.

What We Believe

Three things we never skip

Plain talk

We explain your options in everyday words. You'll always know what we suggest and why, before anything is made.

Made to measure

Your body is not a standard size, so your device should not be either. We build each one to your shape.

Care that continues

Bodies change and children grow. We keep checking the fit long after the first day, so it keeps working for you.

Why Choose Us

Finding it hard to walk, grip or stay comfortable?

Living with limb loss or a weak joint is tiring. The right device can make daily life easier, but only if it fits well and you know how to use it. That's where we put our effort.

Your first visit is mostly a conversation. We ask about your goals, your daily routine and what has been hard. Then we check your limb, your joints and the way you walk or use your hands.

We take a cast or careful measurements and build the device to that shape. You try it before it's finished, so we can fix the fit early instead of after you've gone home.

A new limb or brace takes practice. We teach you how to put it on, how to care for your skin and how to build up the hours you wear it, step by step.

Our Approach

We stay with you long after the fitting

A new limb or brace rarely feels perfect on day one. Your skin settles, your muscles get stronger and you start using the device in new ways. That's normal.

So we don't stop at handover. We check the fit, make small changes and help you get used to it, one visit at a time. The chart shows how each step builds on the last.
